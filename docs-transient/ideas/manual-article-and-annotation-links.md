# Manual links between articles and annotations

Allow the user to manually connect articles and annotations when they recognize a
relationship that semantic search does not surface.

For example, an annotated passage in “Federal pressure may force design education
to rethink its identity” reminded the user of something they read in “Designed
for a Dead Language,” but semantic search was not connecting the two. The user
wants to preserve that connection explicitly.

Possible connections:

- Annotation to article — likely the primary use.
- Annotation to annotation — possibly another primary use.
- Article to annotation.
- Article to article.

Manual links would preserve relationships the user recognizes without depending
on semantic search to rediscover them. They also require the user to choose and
maintain connections. How links are created, displayed, and followed, and whether
they have a direction or appear at both ends, remain open design questions.
